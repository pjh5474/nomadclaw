# Stretching and mobility

## Before lifting (5–8 min)
- World’s greatest stretch ×5/side
- Hip flexor stretch ×30–45 sec/side
- Ankle rocks against a wall ×10/side
- Cat-cow ×8
- Bodyweight squat holds ×2×20 sec

## Before running (5 min)
- Leg swings front/back and side ×10/side
- Walking lunges ×8/side
- Ankle circles ×10/side
- Easy skips or jog 2–3 min

## After sessions (cool-down)
- Hold stretches 30–60 seconds, breathe calmly
- Focus areas: hip flexors, hamstrings, calves, pecs/shoulders after pressing days
- Foam roll quads/glutes lightly if sore — avoid aggressive rolling on acute injuries

## Knee-friendly options
- Prefer open-chain or supported stretches if deep knee flexion hurts
- Limit end-range loaded knee stretch; prioritize hip/ankle mobility instead

## Rules of thumb
- Dynamic mobility before training; longer static holds after
- Stretching is not a substitute for progressive loading and recovery sleep
