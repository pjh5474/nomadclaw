# Running program (5K focus)

## Principles
- Most weekly volume should be easy conversational pace
- Add one quality session per week (intervals or tempo)
- Increase weekly distance by roughly 10% or less
- Rest or cross-train after hard days

## Sample 4-week build toward a 30-minute 5K
### Week 1
- Mon: easy 20–25 min
- Wed: intervals 6×1 min hard / 2 min easy
- Fri: easy 25 min
- Sun: easy 30 min

### Week 2
- Mon: easy 25 min
- Wed: tempo 12–15 min steady
- Fri: easy 25–30 min
- Sun: easy 35 min

### Week 3
- Mon: easy 25–30 min
- Wed: intervals 8×1 min hard / 90 sec easy
- Fri: easy 30 min
- Sun: easy 40 min

### Week 4 (sharpen)
- Mon: easy 20–25 min
- Wed: short tempo 10 min
- Fri: easy 20 min + 4 strides
- Race/test: 5K time trial

## Pacing notes
- Easy: can speak full sentences
- Tempo: comfortably hard, short phrases only
- Intervals: hard but controlled; form stays clean

## Injury flags
- Reduce intensity if knee/shin pain persists more than 48 hours
- Prefer softer surfaces and shorter strides when returning from knee issues
