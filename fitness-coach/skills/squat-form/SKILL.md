# Squat form guide

## Setup
- Feet roughly shoulder-width, toes slightly out
- Bar sits on upper traps (high bar) or rear delts (low bar)
- Brace the core before unrack; take a deep breath into the belly

## Descent
- Break at hips and knees together
- Keep the chest up and the bar path vertical over mid-foot
- Knees track over toes — do not collapse inward
- Aim for at least parallel (hip crease at or below knee) unless injury limits depth

## Ascent
- Drive the floor away; push mid-foot
- Hips and shoulders rise together — avoid early hip shoot
- Exhale after passing the sticking point or at the top

## Red flags
- Heels lifting, excessive forward lean, or knees caving
- Pain (especially sharp knee/low-back pain) — stop and regress load or range

## Coaching cues
- "Screw feet into the floor"
- "Ribs down, brace hard"
- "Knees out over pinky toes"
